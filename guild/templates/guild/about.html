Loot & Some Fun Is Recruiting!

A small, competent, and relaxed raid guild that values teamwork, consistency, and enjoying EverQuest without unnecessary pressure.

Who We Are

Our raid environment is focused and efficient, but never at the expense of having fun. Although the guild is primarily focused on raiding, many of our veteran members gladly help newer players with quests, keying, and experience groups.

What We Offer

  • A relaxed but capable raid atmosphere
  • An open-roll and loot-council system with no DKP
  • Fair and transparent loot distribution that favors mains over alts
  • A welcoming community for veteran and returning players
  • Experienced members willing to help with quests, keys, and leveling

Raid Schedule

Monday, Tuesday, and Friday
7:00 PM–11:00 PM Eastern Time

We often finish ahead of schedule.

Current Raid Progression

We are currently clearing Vex Thal, Emperor Ssraeshza, Lord Inquisitor Seru, and Cursed. We also revisit other targets as needed to help gear our members, such as Sleeper’s Tomb for primal and avatar weapons.

New-Member Probation

New members complete a two-week probationary period. You will still be able to raid, have fun, and receive eligible loot while getting to know the guild.
